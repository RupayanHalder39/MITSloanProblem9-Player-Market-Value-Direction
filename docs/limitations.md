# Limitations

- Market values are published estimates rather than realized transfer fees.
- The exact-value improvement is specific to the Championship test and does not generalize across the wider competition pool.
- Neither model identified the 10 pre-declared extreme breakout rises.
- The Spearman difference is not reliably supported by the player-clustered bootstrap interval.
- Playing-time and feature-family findings are predictive associations, not causal effects.
- Raw data cannot currently be redistributed, limiting independent end-to-end reproduction.
